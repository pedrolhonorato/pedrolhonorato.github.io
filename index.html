About

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at the Graduate Program in Economics (CAEN) at the Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Brazil.

I hold a Master's degree in Economics and a Bachelor's degree in Economics from the Federal University of Ceará.

My research interests include applied macroeconomics, financial economics, applied econometrics, and public economics. I am particularly interested in the application of quantitative methods to economic policy and regional economic issues.

I currently work as a Research Fellow in the Chief Scientist Program at the Ceará State Department of Finance (SEFAZ/CE), conducting applied research on the Brazilian tax reform.

In 2026, I was a visiting student at the IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca, Italy, as part of my doctoral academic activities.
